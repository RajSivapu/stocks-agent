# Stock Agent investment risk disclosure

Last updated: 2026-09-03

Stock Agent is a research and decision-support tool. It is not a broker, investment adviser, fiduciary, exchange, custodian, or trade-execution service. It cannot place, modify, or cancel an order. Every output is a suggestion; you alone decide whether and how to trade.

Investing can result in partial or total loss. Market prices can change materially between a morning brief, an intraday run, your review, and an eventual fill. Quotes, filings, news, corporate-action data, calculations, and third-party services can be delayed, incomplete, unavailable, inconsistent, or wrong. Models can misunderstand evidence, overstate confidence, omit relevant facts, and produce plausible but incorrect analysis.

The service uses deterministic policy checks, evidence references, fresh intraday context, an independent checker, position limits, and fail-closed states to reduce avoidable errors. Those controls do not guarantee accuracy, suitability, performance, liquidity, tax treatment, or protection from loss. Past performance, backtests, social-media claims, analyst opinions, and model confidence do not predict future results.

Before acting, verify the current quote, position and cash balance in your broker, order type, fees, taxes, liquidity, official issuer filings, and any corporate action. Consider your time horizon, concentration, risk tolerance, and need for professional financial or tax advice. Never treat a notification, buy zone, stop level, target, score, or silence from the service as an instruction or guarantee.

The service is **not continuously monitored** and a displayed stop is **not a stop order**. A quiet,
late, failed, or delivery-unknown notification does not mean a position is safe. The Analyst and
Checker are structured passes, but the release-one **same-model Checker** is not an independent model,
licensed analyst, or second professional opinion. Outages, provider usage limits, market halts, gaps,
and fast markets can make every automated check stale before you see it.

You must personally place every trade in your own broker after checking the current account and
market state. Friends and other owners make independent decisions; neither the operator nor another
owner is responsible for a loss, missed opportunity, tax outcome, or unavailable alert.

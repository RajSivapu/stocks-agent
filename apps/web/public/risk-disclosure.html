Past performance, backtests, social-media claims, and model confidence do not predict future results.

Before acting, verify the current quote, broker position and cash balance, order type, fees, taxes, liquidity, issuer filings, and corporate actions. Never treat a notification, buy zone, stop, target, score, or silence as an instruction or guarantee. Return to Stock Agent.

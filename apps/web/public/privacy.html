Stock Agent privacy and data lifecycle

Last updated: September 3, 2026

Stock Agent is invite-only, suggestion-only portfolio research and recordkeeping. It does not request brokerage credentials and cannot place, modify, or cancel brokerage orders.

Data and access

The service stores account and consent records, settings, your transaction ledger and holdings, plans, research evidence, recommendations, run state, provider-connection status, and Telegram delivery state. The operator can access production records and encrypted recovery archives when needed to operate, secure, restore, export, or delete the service. Reusable secrets and raw Telegram identifiers are excluded from user exports.

Other services

Supabase hosts authentication and private application data. Your connected analysis provider receives bounded portfolio and research context. Provider transcripts remain in your provider account under that provider's retention controls; Stock Agent cannot delete them. Market-data and research vendors receive requested ticker symbols. Telegram receives bot messages and delivery identifiers. Cloudflare serves the static application and does not embed portfolio data in it.

Export and deletion

Settings provides owner-bound JSON and immutable-ledger CSV exports. Confirming deletion immediately disconnects analysis and Telegram, disables schedules and notifications, and starts a 72-hour cancellation window. Active data and the Auth identity are deleted no later than seven days after the request. Encrypted recovery archives expire within 35 days. A minimal UUID-and-time tombstone remains to prevent deleted accounts from being restored.

Stock Agent attempts to remove up to 100 eligible bot messages from the preceding 48 hours. Telegram may reject deletion; you must remove older messages manually, and Telegram's own policies apply.

For requests not available in the app, contact the operator through your invitation channel. Return to Stock Agent.
